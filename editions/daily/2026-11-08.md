# Simple human error still costs £100,000 in court

*A High Court negligence order against Ratio Law puts £100,000 on one deleted figure, while the week's AI launches still rest on vendors' own word.*

[REPORTED] A High Court judge sitting in Manchester has ordered Ratio Law to pay MPP (Services) Ltd £100,000 plus interest, Legal Futures reports. The firm admitted that a partner accidentally deleted the sum secured from a draft legal charge on a property. The borrower then argued the charge was void for uncertainty, and MPP accepted it could not answer that.

The judge described the partner as an unimpressive witness who gave the matter only the bare minimum of attention. It is one firm's admitted error in England and Wales, not evidence of a trend.

The Verdict's view: a £100,000 order for one deleted figure is a question of controls rather than technology, and tools that make drafts quicker to edit make a second read matter more, not less.

Name a second reader for every document that secures money, and say what they must check.
