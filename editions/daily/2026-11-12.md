# Weil's vote ends the question; the claims begin

Weil, Gotshal & Manges partners have voted 97 per cent to dissolve the firm, leaving creditors, claims and about 1,200 lawyers' next moves to settle.

[REPORTED] A three-person committee will oversee the wind-down, ABA Journal reports, citing Law.com. Bloomberg reports that the restructuring officers come from AlixPartners and C Street Advisory Group, with Davis Polk advising as legal counsel. Law.com also reports that restructuring chair Matt Barr and several bankruptcy lawyers are in talks to join Skadden, and that the firm lost nearly 50 partners in 2026. The Law Society Gazette says Sidley Austin is in talks to make 200 hires from Weil, mostly lawyers but including some business professionals.

Legal Business reports that Weil is braced for litigation. Albert Togut of Togut Segal & Segal says everyone involved in a law firm failure is a lawyer, so any of them can sue without outside counsel.

The Verdict's view: The vote was the easy part: the wind-down will be judged on its creditor and partner claims, and the firms hiring Weil's lawyers will be watched on what they inherit.

https://abajournal.com/news/article/weil-partners-vote-to-dissolve-firm

[REPORTED] MPs want legislation so that the September High Court ruling in Carter-Ruck does not stop regulators reaching privileged material, Legal Futures reports. Labour MP Phil Brickell, speaking at the second reading of the Financial Services and Markets Bill, warned that wrongdoing by solicitors could go unscrutinised. The bill is not law; in England and Wales it would move AML supervision of lawyers and accountants to the FCA, with transition due to begin in 2028. The Law Society has objected on duplication and cost, and has also asked for safeguards on privilege and confidentiality.

The Verdict's view: Privilege may prove a harder fight than duplication, because MPs asking regulators to see more privileged material put the Law Society's existing safeguards case on the defensive.

Advice: ask your COLP which privileged files a regulator could request, and who decides what is handed over.

https://legalfutures.co.uk/latest-news/mps-call-for-legislation-to-overturn-carter-ruck-privilege-ruling

[ANNOUNCED] Latham & Watkins says it has set aside $1m for 2027 for its Associate AI Impact Award, open to associates in all its offices who build AI tools or improve workflows. Michael Rubin, chair of the firm's AI practice, says the aim is to scale useful ideas, Legal Cheek reports.

The Verdict's view: A bonus pot pays for ideas, not adoption, and the measure is whether winning tools are used by teams other than their authors.
