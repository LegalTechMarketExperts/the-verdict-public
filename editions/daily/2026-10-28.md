# Hidden AI labels on human-written documents deserve a buyer's attention

Greg Lambert and Marlene Gebauer report that Lexis Protégé tags typed Word files as AI-generated, and say a week passed without a vendor announcement.

[REPORTED] Writing on 3 Geeks and a Law Blog, Greg Lambert and Marlene Gebauer report from their own testing that Lexis Protégé exports label Word documents as AI-generated, including files the user typed. They describe five hidden document properties and say a week of internal discussion passed without an announcement from the vendor. [VERIFY: confirm the metadata behaviour with LexisNexis] The account is the authors' testing alone; no vendor response is cited.

The Verdict's view: A wrong provenance flag is a disclosure problem in miniature, so test what your own AI tool writes into a document you typed yourself before a client or court asks.

https://geeklawblog.com/2026/10/lexis-protege-is-labeling-your-downloads-ai-generated-even-the-ones-you-typed-yourself.html
