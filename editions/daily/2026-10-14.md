# Two legal AI rivals launch Intake; the name is the lesser point

Harvey and Legora each unveiled a product called Intake within hours of one another, and the buyer's question is what each does with the work it routes.

Artificial Lawyer reports that Harvey has launched Intake, a front door for in-house teams that routes contracts to the right lawyers. The report indicates it does more than that, but details are limited. Legal Cheek reports that Legora unveiled its own product, also called Intake, within hours. Neither report gives commercial terms, and Legora's launch is not described in any detail.

Our read: the name clash is a bad afternoon for two marketing teams. The pattern matters more. Two leading legal AI vendors are now competing over how in-house work is received and sorted, not only how it is drafted. For law firms, that is where instructions may start to be triaged. A front door is only as good as the rules behind it, and no launch report describes them. Ask who audits the routing decisions an intake tool makes.
