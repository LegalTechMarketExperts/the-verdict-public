# Freshfields reportedly eyes outside capital for its AI unit

*Legal Business, citing the Financial Times, reports that Freshfields is weighing external investment in its AI unit, and the stake is who ends up owning the tools a firm's lawyers rely on.*

Legal Business reports, citing the Financial Times, that Freshfields is considering opening its AI unit to external investment. Leaders are said to be discussing carve-out options that could create a company worth billions. The talks came to light through a leaked internal email; Nonbillable, also citing the FT, says it went by mistake to a firm-wide list. No valuation is given, and both reports rest on one FT account. [VERIFY: single FT-sourced report; check with Freshfields]

Our read: "billions" is the FT's sourcing, not an announced deal. A spun-out unit would be owned by a firm and probably sold first to that firm, with outside investors pressing it to sell widely. For other firms, the question is whether a rival's technology company becomes a supplier or a competitor. Ask any law-firm-owned vendor who controls what your usage teaches its models.
