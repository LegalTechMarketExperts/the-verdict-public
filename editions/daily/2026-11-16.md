# Weil's brand has bidders, but its data is the real question

Crosby has bid for the brand, trademark and selected data of Weil, Gotshal & Manges, and privilege may limit what any buyer can take.

[REPORTED] Since Weil, Gotshal & Manges partners voted on 10 November to dissolve the firm, AI-native firm Crosby has bid for its brand, trademark and selected internal data, TechCrunch reported, as relayed by LawFuel. Scissero and Vetta are also reported to be interested in other assets.

Financial terms are undisclosed, and client confidentiality and privilege may limit what can be acquired. The bidders are AI-native firms, so the appetite for data and precedents is the point, not the name.

The Verdict's view: A name is easy to sell, but data is what an AI-native bidder wants, and privilege will decide how little of it is usable.

https://lawfuel.com/ai-law-firms-circle-the-warm-body-of-weil-gotshal

[REPORTED] Addleshaw Goddard is preparing a no-win, no-fee group action over family protection trusts created by McClure, the collapsed Anglo-Scottish firm, Legal Futures reports. The claim will be brought in Scotland but cover the whole UK, and affected people are invited to register interest. Jones Whyte, which took on McClure's work, reported 18,840 FPT files at the point of administration in 2021.

The Verdict's view: Conditional-fee backing suggests claimant lawyers see enough volume to carry the risk, and an old product line can return as a group claim long after the firm that sold it has gone.

Count the historic files your firm holds on any single product, and know which would sit in a group claim.

[ANNOUNCED] Diligent Institute and Wilson Sonsini say their Q4 2026 General Counsel Risk Index, based on about 200 senior legal leaders, finds nearly 60 per cent believe AI adoption is outpacing governance. They also say 41 per cent lack a basic AI governance framework, and that board AI readiness averages 2.9 out of five. The survey is predominantly North American, and LawFuel relayed the findings.

The Verdict's view: About 200 respondents show a mood rather than a market, but a legal team that cannot show its own framework will find AI budgets harder to defend.
