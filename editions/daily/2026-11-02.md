# Maine's bar changes research supplier, and the terms go unreported

*LawSites and LawNext reports that the Maine bar has moved from Decisis to Clio Library, a small switch that shows how hard research suppliers compete for bar partnerships.*

[REPORTED] LawSites and LawNext reports that the Maine bar has moved from the research service Decisis to Clio Library. The article traces the contest among research platforms for state and local bar partnerships, from Fastcase and Casemaker onwards. It gives no contract terms. [VERIFY: check bar's own announcement for switch date]

The Verdict's view: a bar partnership puts a research tool in front of lawyers by default, which is worth fighting for. Without terms, the switch shows who is chasing distribution, not whose product is better.
