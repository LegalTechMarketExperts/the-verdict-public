# Maine's bar moves to Clio Library: free for members, terms with Clio undisclosed

*LawSites and LawNext report that the Maine bar switched from Decisis to Clio Library on 1 October, free to members and saving the bar more than $50,000 a year; what Clio gets in return is not disclosed.*

[REPORTED] LawSites and LawNext report that the Maine bar has moved from the research service Decisis to Clio Library. The article traces the contest among research platforms for state and local bar partnerships, from Fastcase and Casemaker onwards. The switch took effect on 1 October; the bar says members get the service free and it saves more than $50,000 a year, but the commercial terms with Clio are not disclosed.

The Verdict's view: a bar partnership puts a research tool in front of lawyers by default, which is worth paying for; the bar's saving is on record, and what Clio gets for it is not.

https://lawnext.com/2026/11/after-signing-on-with-upstart-legal-research-service-decisis-maine-bar-switches-to-clio-library.html

[REPORTED] Legal Cheek reports that Weil, Gotshal & Manges' management committee has recommended an orderly wind-down, subject to a partner vote, leaving trainees facing an uncertain future. Citing Bloomberg Law, it says Ramona Nee, the firm's incoming leader, is in advanced talks with Kirkland; the Gibson Dunn private funds link is unsourced. Legal IT Insider has opened a support hub for affected lawyers and business services staff; that is its own announcement, and its contents are not fully described.

The Verdict's view: closures are read for the partners; the staff who run a firm's systems are the quieter story.

[ANNOUNCED] Definely's MCP, launched in April, brings contract review tools into the enterprise AI tools lawyers already use; its newer release is a ChatGPT plugin, dated 25 October. The vendor is the only source, and the only third-party voice is a customer quote supplied by the vendor.

The Verdict's view: putting the tool where the lawyer already works is sensible; six months on, which assistants, on what terms and for which customers is still the vendor's account.

The judgment is Corporation Leasing & Finance Ltd v Irwin Mitchell LLP [2026] EWHC 2785 (KB), handed down on 30 October.

The Verdict's view: for risk and knowledge teams, a record of whose advice was relied on is part of the defence.
