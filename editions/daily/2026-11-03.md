# The NCSC's AI warning is really about cheap technology

*The National Cyber Security Centre says UK critical infrastructure faces AI-enabled attacks within two years, and puts the blame on 'tech on the cheap'.*

[CONFIRMED] The National Cyber Security Centre has warned that UK organisations face AI-enabled attacks on critical infrastructure within two years. According to the NCSC, 'tech on the cheap' has left organisations exposed to AI tools that uncover and exploit long-standing security weaknesses. The agency calls for urgent investment in resilience. This is a warning from the agency, not a rule in force.

The Verdict's view: the sentence that matters is the one about cheap technology, which turns old systems into a purchasing decision rather than an IT chore. Few law firms are critical infrastructure; some of their clients are.

https://ncsc.gov.uk/news/cyber-chief-uk-facing-ai-powered-attacks-on-critical-infrastructure-in-two-years
