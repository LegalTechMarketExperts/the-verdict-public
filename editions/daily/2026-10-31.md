# Legal AI platforms widen their range faster than the evidence

Harvey has acquired the sales execution company Aida, Law360 Pulse reports, but the price, terms and date are not stated.

[REPORTED] Law360 Pulse reports that Harvey, the legal AI company, has acquired Aida, a sales execution company. The report gives no price or terms and does not say when the deal closed. [VERIFY: Confirm deal date, terms and Aida's details with Harvey or a second outlet]

The Verdict's view: Sales tooling sits a long way from drafting and research, so read this as a vendor widening its range, not as proof that the range fits a law firm.

https://law360.com/pulse/legal-tech/articles/2544160

[REPORTED] The ABA Journal reports that a new report warns of rising cyberattacks using agentic AI against law firms and other legal bodies. The report's author, date and figures are not stated. [VERIFY: check the named report, its publisher and its figures]

The Verdict's view: A warning is not a measurement; ask for the figures behind it before it moves a security budget.

https://abajournal.com/news/article/law-firms-facing-increasing-ai-fueled-cyber-attacks-new-report-says

[REPORTED] LawFuel reports that Gibson Dunn is poised to take about 40 lawyers from Weil as London departures accelerate. The practices and timing are not given. [VERIFY: Confirm the 40 figure and the London office detail with Gibson Dunn or Weil] Legal Business and Above the Law have both run commentary treating Weil as a cautionary case.

The Verdict's view: When a team walks, the know-how and matter history it built stay with the firm only if someone planned for it.

https://lawfuel.com/gibson-dunn-poised-to-take-40-weil-lawyers-as-london-exodus-accelerates

[ANNOUNCED] Latham & Watkins says the FCA has issued guidance on firms' use of AI, with examples of good and poor practice. In Latham's account it shows how the existing UK framework applies, rather than adding a new rule; the publication date is not stated. [VERIFY: check guidance against FCA primary document]

The Verdict's view: Regulated clients will measure their suppliers against this, so expect its language to turn up in your next AI questionnaire.

https://lw.com/en/insights/fca-issues-guidance-for-firms-on-their-use-of-ai

[ANNOUNCED] NetDocuments chief executive Josh Baxter says, in a LawNext podcast carried by Legal Tech Monitor, that the document management system is becoming a firm's intelligence layer. He adds that lawyers increasingly work inside Claude, Harvey, Legora and ChatGPT.

The Verdict's view: A document vendor naming itself the centre is describing its own role, not a settled architecture.

https://legaltechmonitor.com/2026/10/lawnext-podcast-netdocs-ceo-josh-baxter-on-why-the-dms-is-becoming-the-law-firms-intelligence-layer

[REPORTED] LawFuel reports that Mary Technology is now available on Anthropic's Claude. Terms, integration scope and the date of availability are not stated. [VERIFY: Confirm availability with Mary Technology or Anthropic]

The Verdict's view: Availability on an assistant is a listing, not a deployment; find out what the integration actually does.

https://lawfuel.com/legal-tech-news-mary-technology-now-available-on-claude
