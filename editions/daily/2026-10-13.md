# The SRA asks what AI means for solicitor competence

*The SRA has opened an eight week consultation on how technology and AI should shape solicitors' learning and development, with the Statement of Solicitor Competence in scope.*

The SRA has launched an eight week consultation on updates to the Statement of Solicitor Competence and functioning legal knowledge, reported by Today's Wills and Probate. It opened on 8 October and closes on 3 December 2026. The SRA says technology and AI are changing how solicitors learn and develop, and it asks for views on the proposed changes. The proposals are not in force. They would affect solicitors in England and Wales and the firms that train them. The SRA has published the proposed text as Annex 1 on its consultation page, and intends to publish the final version in April 2027 for effect in September 2027.

Our read: a competence statement is where regulatory expectation becomes firm policy. If AI is written into it, training records, supervision and tool approval follow. Nothing changes until the SRA publishes a final text, but firms that respond will shape it and firms that do not will inherit it. For a managing partner or COO: test whether your current AI training would survive being read against a competence standard.
